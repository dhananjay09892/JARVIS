WHILE IT WAITS FOR "HEY JARVIS"
After you click INITIALISE, JARVIS keeps the microphone open until you close
it. By default an offline wake word (openWakeWord) listens for "Hey Jarvis" on
your computer, so while JARVIS is asleep what the microphone hears stays on
your computer.
Once it hears its name, what you say is sent to a speech service to be turned
into text:
- With an ElevenLabs key, to ElevenLabs through the JARVIS bridge.
- Without a key, to your browser's own speech service. In Google Chrome that
  is Google; in Microsoft Edge it is Microsoft.
If you turn the offline wake word off in Settings, or it cannot start on your
computer, JARVIS falls back to cloud listening and shows a CLOUD LISTENING
badge. Then, to notice "Hey Jarvis", sound heard while it is asleep (including
a TV or music) is sent to the same speech service, so room sound can leave
your computer before you wake JARVIS. Close JARVIS when you do not want it
listening.

YOUR DATA
Your settings, keys and memories are stored on your own computer. What you say
to JARVIS is sent to the services you connect (for example Claude, and
ElevenLabs if you add a key), under their own privacy terms.

MESSAGING JARVIS FROM TELEGRAM (OFF UNLESS YOU TURN IT ON)
If you turn on Settings > Channels > Telegram and add your own bot's token:
- What leaves this computer: your messages to the bot and JARVIS's replies,
  anything you send the bot (voice notes, photos, files), and your Telegram
  user id. JARVIS does not download voice notes, photos or files yet; it only
  reads typed text. The brain you use (for example Claude) sees the text of
  each message for that turn, as it does with speech.
- Who can read it: Telegram. Chats with a bot are ordinary Telegram cloud
  chats, which are not end-to-end encrypted
  (https://tsf.telegram.org/manuals/e2ee-simple).
- How long Telegram keeps it: messages stay in Telegram's cloud until you
  delete them. Messages waiting for JARVIS to collect them are kept by
  Telegram for at most 24 hours (https://core.telegram.org/bots/api).
- How to delete it: delete the chat in Telegram (for both sides), revoke the
  pairing in Settings > Channels, and delete the bot with @BotFather.
- What stays here: who is paired, in JARVIS's local data folder. People you
  have not paired get no answer at all. Sending "freeze" to the bot stops all
  of JARVIS's actions and his chat replies; only the app on this computer can
  unfreeze him.

VOICEPRINTS (SPEAKER RECOGNITION)
Speaker recognition is off by default. When you turn it on and enrol someone,
JARVIS turns a few seconds of their speech into a list of numbers (a
voiceprint) and uses it to tell who is talking. A voiceprint is biometric
data, so:
- Only the person being enrolled, or their parent or guardian, should agree
  to it. Enrolment asks for that agreement first.
- Voiceprints and the audio they come from stay on this computer, in JARVIS's
  local data folder. They are never sent to a model, a service or anyone else.
- The only thing downloaded is the recognition model itself (about 29 MB,
  WeSpeaker CAM++, once, from GitHub).
- Deleting a person deletes all of their voiceprints; nothing is kept.
- A voice match never approves anything by itself. Actions that matter still
  need your click, and a voice can be imitated or recorded.
