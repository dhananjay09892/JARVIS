WHILE IT WAITS FOR "HEY JARVIS"
The wake word is not detected on your computer yet. After you click INITIALISE,
JARVIS keeps the microphone open until you close it, and to notice "Hey Jarvis"
it has to send what the microphone hears to a speech service to be turned into
text, even while it is asleep:
- With an ElevenLabs key, each stretch of sound loud enough to be speech
  (including a TV or music) is sent to ElevenLabs through the JARVIS bridge.
- Without a key, your browser's own speech service receives the microphone
  audio continuously. In Google Chrome that is Google; in Microsoft Edge it is
  Microsoft.
So room sound can leave your computer before you wake JARVIS. An offline
wake-word engine is planned. Until then, close JARVIS when you do not want it
listening.
