Eight 2.5-second clips (16 kHz, mono, 16-bit little-endian PCM) of two different synthetic
voices, `slt` and `kal`, four sentences each. Made with the CMU Flite voices (BSD-style
licence) through ffmpeg's `flite` filter; used by `bridge/speaker-campp.test.mjs`. No real
person's voice is in this repository.
